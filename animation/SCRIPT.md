# Six to Picnic — script (v2, story draft)

**Logline.** A little cricket ball spends its whole day being used by other people's games: bowled,
smashed out of the ground, carried off as a stowaway at 300 km/h. It finally rolls into a quiet picnic,
where for the first time somebody actually *needs* it. 15 seconds, no dialogue, one continuous shot.

**Why the three worlds belong together.** The ball is the only thing that passes through all three.
Cricket uses it, racing carries it, the picnic welcomes it. Each handover is also a match-cut
(ball arc → car launch, flag → blanket), so the story and the editing say the same thing: one small
thing travelling until it finds where it fits.

**Protagonist device.** The ball has a tiny upright face (the seam spins behind it, the face stays
level, classic cartoon rule). Its expressions carry the emotion while the big characters carry the action.

**Theme in one line.** Being passed around is not the same as being wanted; the ball ends the day as
the one thing holding the picnic together.
